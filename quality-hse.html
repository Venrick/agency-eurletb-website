Pillar 01

Quality Policy

At EURL ETB Achouri Toufik, quality is an integral part of our corporate identity. We actively structure our operational processes and quality management system around international standards.

Active Strategic Initiative

Pursuing ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Alignment

We are actively implementing comprehensive Quality Management System (QMS) frameworks designed to align directly with the ISO 9001:2015 standard. This ongoing journey reflects our structured commitment to systematic operational control, continuous improvement, and standardized execution across all building construction, public works, and hydraulics projects.

Standard Target ISO 9001:2015 Alignment In Progress

Core Sector Reliability

Guaranteeing flawless execution, technical precision, and structural durability across building construction, public works, and hydraulics (BTPH).

Rigorous Compliance

Strict adherence to client specifications, regulatory legal frameworks, national technical codes, and contractual standards on every project.

Operational Jobsite Control

Enforcing standardized quality checks, material sampling, and phase-by-phase site supervision to eliminate defects before handover.

Staff Competency Development

Continuous professional development and skill elevation for our engineers, site supervisors, and field technicians to maintain technical excellence.

Continuous QMS Improvement

Regularly auditing and refining our quality workflows to enhance operational productivity, lower risks, and elevate client satisfaction.

Durable Relationships

Establishing transparent, long-term relationships with clients, suppliers, and partners based on trust, mutual accountability, and value.

Risk & Opportunity Management

Integrating proactive risk assessment and opportunity analysis across all operational decision-making. By identifying potential project constraints early and capitalizing on technical innovations, we safeguard project budgets, timelines, and quality benchmarks.

Pillar 02

HSE Policy (Health, Safety & Environment)

We are dedicated to establishing and maintaining safe, healthy, and environmentally responsible worksites for all personnel, subcontractors, clients, and site visitors across every job location.

Legal HSE Compliance

Strict compliance with national health, safety, and environmental legislation, regulatory guidelines, and applicable construction codes.

Risk Identification & Control

Systematic hazard evaluation, site risk assessments, and implementation of control measures before work commences on any site.

Accident & Incident Prevention

Relentless pursuit of a zero-incident environment through rigorous safety protocols, proactive hazard reporting, and rapid corrective action.

Health & Workplace Protection

Protecting physical health and ergonomics for workers, subcontractors, visitors, and clients through complete PPE mandates and medical safeguards.

Environmental Impact Reduction

Minimizing ecological footprint through pollution prevention, responsible site waste management, efficient resource utilization, and eco-friendly construction.

Staff HSE Training Culture

Fostering continuous safety awareness through site inductions, mandatory toolbox talks, emergency response drills, and active employee engagement.
