Design

From Concept to Construction Documentation

Our architectural design service covers the full design spectrum — feasibility studies, concept design, detailed architectural drawings, and technical documentation. We turn your requirements into a design that is buildable, compliant, and aligned with national and international engineering codes.

Working alongside structural and MEP engineers, we coordinate every drawing so the design translates cleanly into construction, reducing risk and cost on site.

What's Included

Every Design Project Includes

A complete design and documentation scope for a buildable project.

Concept Design & Feasibility

Early design options, feasibility studies, and spatial planning.

Detailed Architectural Drawings

Plan, elevation, section, and detail drawings to construction standard.

Structural Coordination

Integration of structural layouts and load paths into the architectural design.

Permitting Documentation

Complete technical files prepared for permits and approvals.

Materials & Finishes Specification

Schedules and specifications for materials, finishes, and joinery.

3D Visualisation & BIM

Visualisations and BIM coordination that clarify the design before build.
